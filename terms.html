1 · What the app is

Cracker is a personal, offline record-keeping tool for credit cards. It arranges and calculates over figures you enter and figures read from statement files you provide. It is not a banking service, not a payment service, not a financial product, and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any bank, card network or rewards programme.

It is offered as-is by an individual developer. There is no company behind it, no service operated on your behalf, and no server that holds anything of yours.
